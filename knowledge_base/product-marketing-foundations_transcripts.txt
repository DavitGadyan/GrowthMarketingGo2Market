All right, let's get serious now about those marketing fundamentals. This one has been around for a while. It's called the hierarchy of effects, but it so still resonates today. Then what you're working to do in the marketing effort, what you're working to do is to drive people through this hierarchy. You go from totally unaware of the product to, all right, awareness.
